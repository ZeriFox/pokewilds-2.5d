gdx-gltf 2.1.0 - local PokeWilds PC compatibility patch, 2026-10-05

The bundled library contains a replacement of MeshSpliter that partitions
unsigned-index geometry at complete primitive boundaries, preserving vertex
records and primitive order. It fixes out-of-range indices and broken triangles
in large GLB meshes. The rest of the upstream JAR entries are unchanged.
