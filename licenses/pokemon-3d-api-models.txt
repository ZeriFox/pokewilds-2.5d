Exact URLs, byte-for-byte source hashes, animation/static status and any
embedded artist/source/license credits are recorded per model in MODELS-PROVENANCE.json.
docs/CREDITS.md points to Sketchfab collections rather than a complete per-model
attribution list. Embedded asset.extras credits are retained when supplied.
docs/COPYRIGHT.md explicitly limits the repository's MIT license to code,
excluding rights to Pokemon intellectual property. Any embedded model license
is recorded as an upstream claim, not a grant of underlying Pokemon rights.
Models are unmodified; regular forms are not substituted for regional variants.
