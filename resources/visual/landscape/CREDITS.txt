LOCAL LANDSCAPE ASSETS
Original downloaded packs are preserved outside the project. This atlas is an integration for this local game, not an asset pack.
Pixel Crawler supplies the common material/object language; Woods and supplied Forest_1 supply nature; Cold Cave supplies cave details. Sevarihk supplies the secondary volcanic stone/lava material set. Two CC0 marionline gravestones and one CC0 ScratchIO cactus provide missing silhouettes. Desert, volcano and graveyard use dedicated source-derived palette families.
